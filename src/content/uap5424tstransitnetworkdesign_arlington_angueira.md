## Course Description
This interactive two-day course provides transit and planning professionals with a foundational understanding of public transit network design and hands-on experience working with network design tools. Participants work in small groups to design transit networks for fictional cities using constrained budgets and a defined set of service types, uncovering core trade-offs and principles through structured exercises and debriefing sessions. The course aims to develop an intuitive, craft-level fluency with transit network design as it relates to urban planning and transit-oriented development.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the fundamental trade-offs in transit network design, including frequency, coverage, capital investment, and service type choices
- Gain hands-on, practical experience designing transit networks within budget constraints using spreadsheet-based tools
- Evaluate network outcomes by calculating coverage standards, sample travel times, and other performance measures
- Connect transit network design principles to broader urban planning concerns, including social justice, land use, and development patterns
- Understand the relationship between network design choices and the feasibility of truly transit-oriented development

## Course Topics and Schedule
- **Introduction to Network Design Tools:** Basic exercises using simplified fictional city to introduce core trade-offs in transit network design
- **Network Design Games – Newport:** Small-group exercises using a more realistic city scenario with budget spreadsheets and multiple service type options
- **Evaluating Basic Outcomes:** Testing and measuring network performance through coverage standards, sample travel times, and comparative "races" among group networks
- **Debriefing and Comparative Analysis:** Cross-group comparison of network solutions to derive key principles of transit network design
- **Lecture and Discussion – Network Design Principles:** Connecting transit design theory to broader urban planning contexts including equity, noise, views, and community concerns
- **Bus Networks and Long-Term Options:** Exploring how modal choices, technology, and capital investment levels shape network design decisions
- **Conclusion – Transit and Development Patterns:** Discussion of what discovered principles mean for how transit can and cannot respond to development, and the nature of truly transit-oriented development
