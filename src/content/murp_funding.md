## Virginia Tech internal funding

### Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA)
- **Stipend:** ~$18,000–$20,000/year (verify current rate with Kelly Crist)
- **Tuition:** In-state tuition covered; out-of-state differential may apply
- **Hours:** 20 hrs/week teaching support
- **Eligibility:** Full-time MURP students in good standing; typically awarded year 1 → year 2 continuation
- **Application:** Contact Kelly Crist — kcrist@vt.edu — by Oct 15 (for spring) or Mar 15 (for fall)
- **Competition:** Competitive; not all students receive GTA support
- **Focus match:** Any focus area — GTA duties assigned to UAP undergraduate courses

### Graduate Research Assistantship (GRA)
- **Stipend:** Varies by project (~$15,000–$22,000/year)
- **Tuition:** Often covered depending on grant terms
- **Hours:** 20 hrs/week on faculty research project
- **Eligibility:** Match to a faculty member's funded project
- **Application:** Rolling — contact faculty directly whose research aligns with your interests
- **Focus match:** Transportation (contact transportation faculty), environmental (contact environmental faculty), housing (contact housing faculty)
- **Jane's note:** GRAs are not posted centrally. Students should email faculty in their focus area early in semester 1 to express interest.

### VT Graduate School Fellowships
- **Types:** Doctoral, SPIA-specific, diversity fellowships
- **Amount:** Varies ($5,000–$25,000)
- **Deadline:** January 15 annually
- **URL:** graduateschool.vt.edu/funding
- **Focus match:** All focus areas; some fellowships target underrepresented groups

## National planning scholarships

### APA Planning Fellowship
- **Amount:** $4,000–$8,000
- **Deadline:** March 1 annually
- **Eligibility:** Full-time graduate students in accredited planning programs; AICP candidate status preferred
- **Focus match:** Any — but applications are stronger with a clear policy focus and community engagement narrative
- **URL:** planning.org/scholarships/apa-planning-fellowships
- **Apply via:** planning.org

### AICP Scholarship
- **Amount:** $5,000
- **Deadline:** February 15 annually
- **Eligibility:** Students pursuing AICP certification track
- **URL:** planning.org/scholarships

### Dwight David Eisenhower Transportation Fellowship (DDETFP)
- **Amount:** Up to $10,500/year
- **Deadline:** February (check FHWA website for current year)
- **Eligibility:** Transportation focus; US citizens or permanent residents
- **Focus match:** Transportation planning, infrastructure, transit
- **URL:** eisenhowerfellowships.dot.gov

### HUD Doctoral Dissertation Research Grant
- **Amount:** Up to $25,000
- **Deadline:** Rolling — check HUD website
- **Eligibility:** Doctoral students (note: MURP is masters — not directly applicable, but worth knowing for PhD aspirants)
- **Focus match:** Housing, community development, urban policy

### Robert Wood Johnson Foundation Health Policy Fellows
- **Amount:** Full salary + benefits (~$90,000)
- **Deadline:** August (for following year)
- **Eligibility:** Early-career professionals including recent graduates — more relevant post-MURP
- **Focus match:** Health equity, social determinants, community planning

## Virginia-specific funding

### Virginia Department of Transportation (VDOT) Graduate Research Program
- **Amount:** $15,000–$30,000/project
- **Eligibility:** Students working with a faculty PI on VDOT-approved research topics
- **Focus match:** Transportation planning, traffic engineering, safety
- **Contact:** Through faculty advisor — not a direct student application

### Virginia Housing Research Grant
- **Amount:** Varies
- **Focus match:** Housing policy, affordable housing, housing finance
- **Contact:** Kelly Crist for current opportunities
