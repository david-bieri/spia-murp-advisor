## Course Description
UAP 5224 addresses the data acquisition, measurement, and analytical skills essential to urban and regional planning practice and research. Topics include data collection, sampling, survey design, data quality, descriptive statistics, probability distributions, inferential statistics, and an introduction to emerging technologies for urban planning. The course also covers working with census data and other public-domain datasets, with an emphasis on data manipulation, merging, and restructuring using MS Excel, and includes attention to ethics in planning methods.

---

## Learning Objectives
- Distinguish qualitative and quantitative data, and qualitative and quantitative analytical methods in urban planning and analysis
- Distinguish random and non-random sampling methods
- Conduct and evaluate survey research
- Identify, retrieve, and prepare data for urban and regional planning analysis
- Apply basic quantitative techniques to analyze and present data pertaining to urban and regional planning
- Apply research ethics in data collection, analysis, and reporting

## Course Topics and Schedule

- **Introduction and Overview:** Course objectives, requirements, and introduction to data analysis (Week 1)
- **Data Collection — Sampling and Measurement:** Random vs. non-random sampling, levels of measurement, data quality (Weeks 2–3)
- **Survey Design:** Designing and evaluating survey instruments; introduction to QuestionPro (Week 4)
- **Descriptive Statistics:** Summarizing and visualizing data using statistics (Week 5)
- **Working with Census Data:** Accessing, manipulating, and analyzing census and public-domain datasets (Weeks 6–7)
- **Inferential Statistics and Normal Distribution:** Probability distributions, standard error, confidence intervals, introduction to inferential statistics (Weeks 9–11; includes Assignment 3 poster presentation in Week 10)
- **Hypothesis Testing:** Logic and application of hypothesis tests (Week 12)
- **Comparing Groups and Analyzing Frequency Data:** Two-group comparisons, chi-square, count/frequency data (Weeks 13–14)
- **Investigating Relationships:** Correlation and introduction to regression (Week 15)
- **Review and Final Examination:** Course review and take-home final exam (Week 16)

## Advising Notes
UAP 5224 (Zhang section) is the Blacksburg offering of the required MURP quantitative methods sequence and is well suited for students who need a strong foundation in data collection, descriptive statistics, and inferential analysis using Excel before advancing to more complex methods. It serves as a direct prerequisite for UAP 5494 (Advanced Quantitative Techniques in Planning Research), and MURP students should plan to take these two courses in sequence. The course assumes basic comfort with arithmetic and algebra but does not require prior statistics coursework, making it accessible to students early in the program. Blacksburg-campus MURP students should note that equivalent sections are offered at Arlington/NCR under different instructors (Buehler, Rodriguez), and students should enroll in the section corresponding to their campus.
