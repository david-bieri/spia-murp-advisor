## Course Description
UAP 5424: Urban Futures is a 1-credit intensive weekend module introducing students to futures studies and foresight practices in relationship with urban planning and related fields of policy, management, and design. The course explores what is possible, probable, and desirable for cities and human settlements over the next twenty years — the mid-term future. It connects thematically with SPIA 4454: Future of Cities (3 credits), which students from across disciplines are encouraged to take separately.

---

## Learning Objectives
- Identify key forces and factors that might shape cities and human settlements in the 21st century and mid-term future
- Draw from different frameworks for thinking about the future(s) and considering trends and forecasts, as suggested by Dator, Sardar, Sweeney, and others
- Identify key individuals and institutions currently involved with futures studies and strategic foresight work
- Utilize tools associated with futures studies and foresight work in relationship with urban planning, policy, design, and related fields

## Advising Notes
UAP 5424: Urban Futures is a 1-credit intensive weekend module well suited for MURP students interested in futures studies, strategic foresight, and long-range urban planning — particularly those exploring how planners can anticipate and shape cities over the next 20 years. The course complements the broader MURP curriculum by introducing foresight methodologies (scenario planning, causal layered analysis, megatrend analysis) that can enrich thesis, studio, or policy work across concentrations. There are no stated prerequisites, and the course is explicitly open to students from multiple disciplines; MURP students are also encouraged to consider pairing it with SPIA 4454: Future of Cities (3 credits) for deeper engagement with the topic. The course is delivered entirely online-synchronously as a weekend intensive, making it accessible regardless of a student's home campus, though it is offered through the Arlington/NCR program under instructor Tirpak.
