## Course Description
UAP 5125 is a client-based planning studio in which students work individually and in collaborative groups on real-world community projects for the City of Alexandria. Students gain skills in project management, problem identification, data collection and analysis, community and stakeholder engagement, and professional norms and practices. Studio projects for Fall 2024 focus on three transportation topics: an E-Bike Lending Library proposal, a Citywide Speed Limit Analysis, and a No Right Turn on Red Analysis.

## Learning Objectives
- Assess the strengths and weaknesses of different types of data
- Design and implement data/information collection programs
- Analyze data and policy-relevant information
- Effectively communicate research findings, both orally and in writing

## Advising Notes
This studio course is well suited for MURP students with interests in transportation planning, active transportation, Vision Zero, and community-based applied research, particularly those seeking hands-on experience working with a municipal government client. It fits naturally into the MURP curriculum as a capstone-style applied learning experience, providing professional practice in project management, stakeholder engagement, data analysis, and policy communication. The course is offered at the Arlington/NCR (Ballston) campus and is taught in person most weeks, making it most accessible to students based in the Northern Virginia/DC area. Students should be aware that significant work occurs outside of class time in field and team settings, and that IRB certification must be completed within the first two weeks of the semester.
