## Course Description
Volunteers are increasingly important in the United States and around the world, and many nonprofit and public organizations rely on them to enhance capacity and address public problems. However, organizations frequently struggle to manage volunteers effectively — both meeting volunteers' needs and retaining high-quality contributors. This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the design and management of volunteer programs.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the motivations behind volunteerism
- Develop strategies for recruiting and retaining volunteers
- Analyze case studies of successful volunteer engagement
- Implement community engagement practices in real-world settings
- Evaluate the impact of volunteer programs on organizations and communities

## Course Topics and Schedule
- **Introduction to Volunteerism:** Overview of volunteerism, community involvement, and the role of volunteers in society (Week 1)
- **Volunteering Concepts and Strategic Volunteer Management:** Defining volunteering, volunteer management frameworks (Weeks 2–3)
- **Motivations and Types of Volunteering:** Volunteer motivations, determinants of volunteering, special considerations for volunteer boards (Week 3)
- **Organizational Needs, Recruiting, and Socialization:** Organizational culture, resource planning, orientation, training, and onboarding processes (Weeks 4–5)
- **Interpersonal Dynamics and Volunteer Retention:** Psychological contracts, staff-volunteer relationships, recognition and reward systems (Weeks 6–7)
- **Equity, Technology, and Inclusive Engagement:** Strategies for inclusive volunteer engagement, addressing barriers for underrepresented communities, digital tools and virtual volunteering (Weeks 8–9)
- **Evaluation of Volunteer Programs:** Measuring impact, monitoring and evaluation frameworks, tools for assessing program effectiveness (Week 10)
- **Community Partnerships and Strategic Planning:** Building organizational partnerships, reviewing strategic volunteer management approaches, case studies of collaboration (Weeks 11–12)
- **Challenges and Future Trends:** Conflict resolution, crisis management, emerging trends, and the impact of social change on volunteering (Weeks 13–14)
- **Final Presentations and Course Wrap-Up:** Presentation of strategic volunteer management plans, course reflection, careers in nonprofit management (Week 15)
