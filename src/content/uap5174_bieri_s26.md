## Course Overview

Overview of key planning theories, planning history, and planning processes and
approaches. Equips students with conceptual tools to improve the quality of
deliberation and a critical perspective on contemporary urban planning challenges.

## Prerequisites

No formal prerequisites. Some exposure to undergraduate-level urban history is
useful but not required.

## Weekly Reflections (40%)

- Required: 12 reflections out of 14 possible weeks (Weeks 2–15)
- May skip up to 2 weeks without penalty
- Length: approximately 400 words each
- Due: Fridays at 5:00pm
- Final deadline for all reflections: May 2 (Week 15)
- Submission: Canvas (private — not visible to other students)
- Late submissions: not accepted except in documented emergencies

Reflections are NOT summaries. They must demonstrate critical engagement and
original thinking. Each reflection should do one or more of the following:
- Identify key tensions or contradictions between different readings
- Connect theoretical concepts to contemporary planning challenges
- Raise questions or critiques to explore in class discussion
- Apply planning theories to cases beyond those in the readings

## AI Use Policy

This course takes a balanced approach to AI tools.

### Permitted uses
- Brainstorming ideas and exploring different perspectives on planning issues
- Editing and improving clarity of your own writing (grammar, structure, flow)
- Generating summaries of readings to check your own comprehension
- Creating outlines or organizing your thoughts
- Researching background information or definitions

### Prohibited uses
- Generating original analysis, arguments, or interpretations for assignments
- Writing substantial portions of weekly reflections, debate arguments, or final papers
- Substituting AI-generated content for engagement with course readings
- Using AI to produce work you represent as your own critical thinking

### Required disclosure
Any use of AI tools must be documented in a brief note at the end of your assignment.
Example: "I used Perplexity to help reorganize my outline and improve sentence
clarity in paragraphs 3–5."

Undisclosed use of AI for prohibited purposes is a violation of the Graduate Honor Code.
