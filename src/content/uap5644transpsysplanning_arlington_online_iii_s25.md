## Course Description
This course provides an overview of urban transportation planning in the United States with a focus on local and community-level transport planning. Transportation planning is guided by federal, state, and local laws and regulations, with policies directed at mobility, accessibility, safety, social equity, and economic development. Transportation systems planning facilitates the information needed for decision makers to choose appropriate investment strategies and policy directives.

## Learning Objectives
- Become familiar with the fundamentals and principles of modern multi-modal transportation planning through practical and theoretical approaches
- Gain knowledge in modern transportation planning policy through understanding of federal, state, regional, and local regulatory laws and policies
- Develop an understanding of planning for urban multi-modal systems
- Critically evaluate policies and plans directed at improving the current transportation system

## Course Topics and Schedule
- **Introduction & Foundations:** Overview of transportation/transport planning concepts and their meaning in practice
- **Transportation Systems Planning:** Principles, history, and theoretical frameworks of transportation planning
- **Legal and Regulatory Framework:** Federal, state, and local laws governing transportation planning, including the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law (IIJA)
- **Regional and Long-Range Transportation Planning:** Metropolitan planning organizations (MPOs), regional plans, land use and transportation integration
- **Site-Specific Land Development and Transportation:** Zoning, traffic generators, traffic impact analysis, development applications
- **Safety Considerations in Transportation Planning:** Vision Zero, Safe Systems Approach, safety impact analysis, multimodal safety engineering
- **Transportation Systems Planning in the DMV:** Guest lectures on site analysis, traffic engineering, and DDOT Vision Zero; group activity
- **Multi-Modal Transportation Planning and Urban Design:** Complete Streets, curbside management, bicycle and pedestrian accommodations, quick-build transit projects
- **Plan Implementation:** Strategies and processes for implementing transportation plans

## Advising Notes
UAP 5644 Transportation Systems Planning is well suited for MURP students interested in transportation planning, land use–transportation connections, or urban policy, and would be particularly relevant for students pursuing careers in planning agencies, metropolitan planning organizations, or transportation consulting. The course is delivered fully online-synchronously via Zoom, making it accessible to students across campuses, though the instructor is affiliated with Arlington/NCR and the course content draws heavily on DMV-area examples and guest practitioners. No formal prerequisites are listed, but students with a background or interest in land use planning, policy, or urban systems will find the course most immediately applicable. The practitioner-oriented approach — including guest lectures, group activities, and applied assignments analyzing real cities and development applications — makes this a strong complement to more quantitative or methods-focused MURP coursework.
