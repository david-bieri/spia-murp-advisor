## Course Description
This module focuses on the basics of effective graphic communication for planners, including color theory, font choice and legibility, and the art of telling a cohesive story through visualization. Students learn from professional planning examples across different media and practice design principles through exercises and critiques. The course develops students' ability to read, label, and design graphs, charts, aerials, 2D maps, and diagrams for use throughout their planning coursework and careers.

## Learning Objectives
- Develop a common language of design principles and best practices applicable to planning graphics
- Understand and apply the Elements of Art (line, shape, form, space, color, texture, motion, time) and Principles of Design (unity and variety, balance, emphasis, rhythm, scale, proportion) in a planning context
- Identify intended audiences and determine appropriate messages for graphic visualizations
- Read, critique, and edit common planning graphics such as graphs, infographics, maps, sections/elevations, and 3D perspectives
- Apply basic graphic design considerations including layout, composition, and typography
- Gain familiarity with software tools typically used by planners to generate graphics
