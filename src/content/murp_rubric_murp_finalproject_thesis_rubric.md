# MURP Thesis and Final Project Evaluation Rubric

## Document Overview
This rubric is used to evaluate MURP major papers, practicums, and theses across nine dimensions of academic and professional performance. Each dimension is scored on a 1–5 point scale, with a maximum total of 45 points, and results map to letter grades and pass/fail outcomes.

---

## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Introduction, Problem Statement, or Hypothesis
**Weight:** 5 points maximum
**What is assessed:** The clarity, logic, and evidentiary support of the thesis statement or research question, and how well the introduction orients the reader to the project's rationale and structure.

**Excellent (5 points):** Introduction poses a clear thesis statement or research question thoroughly supported by evidence, examples, observations, and appropriate citations. It effectively presents the nature and structure of the overall paper.

**Adequate (3 points):** Introduction poses a thesis statement or research question with some evidentiary support, but may not fully explain the project's rationale, relevance to the discipline, or overall structure.

**Inadequate (1 point):** Thesis statement or research question is unevenly or illogically supported. Introduction does not clearly explain the nature, structure, rationale, or disciplinary relevance of the capstone.

---

### 2. Familiarity with and Use of Planning Literature and/or the Current State of the Discipline/Profession
**Weight:** 5 points maximum
**What is assessed:** The depth and quality of engagement with relevant academic literature, professional sources, and disciplinary expertise, including recency and appropriateness of citations.

**Excellent (5 points):** Displays impressive mastery of the literature or discipline; engages substantively and productively with sources and experts/professionals. Cites sources appropriate to the discipline with a focus on recent developments in both professional and academic literature.

**Adequate (3 points):** Displays familiarity with a reasonable range of literature and the discipline; demonstrates appropriate grounding in the literature and engagement with experts/professionals.

**Inadequate (1 point):** Large gaps and little grounding in the literature. No substantive engagement with the discipline. Does not present an adequate array of scholarly ideas or professional practice.

---

### 3. Research Methods/Approach
**Weight:** 5 points maximum
**What is assessed:** The appropriateness, rigor, and independence of the research design, data collection protocols, and analytical methods, including IRB compliance where required.

**Excellent (5 points):** Research design and methods represent a thoughtful and creative approach to addressing the research question, such as adoption of interdisciplinary methods. Student independently develops protocols for data collection and analysis, including IRB review and approval, and demonstrates effective use of available resources.

**Adequate (3 points):** Research design and methods are appropriate to answer the research question. Student employs appropriate protocols for data collection and analysis based on existing models and available data.

**Inadequate (1 point):** Student relies on committee and/or client to outline design, methods, and protocols. Gaps or weaknesses in the method create limitations for the entire paper or project.

---

### 4. Overall Organization of Ideas
**Weight:** 5 points maximum
**What is assessed:** The clarity, consistency, and logic of the argument's structure, including how well sources and multiple perspectives are integrated and how effectively context is used.

**Excellent (5 points):** Argument is clear, consistent, and rigorously logical, with strong organizational links. Sources are used highly effectively to provide supporting detail. The paper relates various sources both to each other and to the main thesis, with context as a critical aspect of the argument.

**Adequate (3 points):** Argument is appropriate, clearly presented, and consistently applied, with good connections among ideas. Occasional organizational problems exist but do not damage overall effectiveness. Integrates multiple viewpoints and presents logical conclusions.

**Inadequate (1 point):** Poorly formulated or absent argument with inadequate supporting detail. Paragraphs are poorly organized and illogical. Does not consider context.

---

### 5. Summary, Conclusions, and Recommendations
**Weight:** 5 points maximum
**What is assessed:** The quality and originality of the conclusions and recommendations, including how well they are grounded in findings, acknowledge limitations, and offer direction for future work or practice.

**Excellent (5 points):** Summary presents carefully analyzed information leading to inventive, originally developed conclusions supported by results. It expands rather than merely repeats prior content, identifies limitations, and points to future research directions. Where appropriate, recommendations address both client-specific issues and broader audiences.

**Adequate (3 points):** Summary is sufficiently supported by results and adequately summarizes the paper or project. Where appropriate, recommendations are generally relevant to practice and to the client.

**Inadequate (1 point):** Summary is minimally supported by results; lacks original ideas, personal interpretation of findings, or an ability to draw an inventive synopsis.

---

### 6. Bibliography and Use of References
**Weight:** 5 points maximum
**What is assessed:** The quality, breadth, currency, relevance, and proper formatting of sources, as well as the integrity of citation practices.

**Excellent (5 points):** Information drawn from a wide array of quality electronic and print sources, including appropriate licensed databases. Sources are current, relevant, balanced, and include critical readings. Primary sources included where appropriate. Bibliography is complete and correctly formatted in an acceptable style (e.g., APA), with citations inserted to validate evidence.

**Adequate (3 points):** Contains a variety of relevant print and electronic sources of varying quality. Complete source documentation is provided, though it may not strictly follow a standard style.

**Inadequate (1 point):** Documentation is poorly constructed or absent. Sources lack relevance, quality, depth, and balance. Evidence of possible plagiarism.

---

### 7. Written and Visual Presentation in the Paper/Project
**Weight:** 5 points maximum
**What is assessed:** The clarity, professionalism, and effectiveness of writing and visual communication, including grammar, mechanics, and use of communication tools.

**Excellent (5 points):** Writing is very strong, concise, and clear—at or near professional level—and free of errors in spelling, grammar, and mechanics. Makes effective and creative use of multiple, appropriate communication tools. Product displays creativity and originality.

**Adequate (3 points):** Writing is reasonably clear; errors in spelling, grammar, and mechanics exist but do not seriously interfere with understandability. Results are effectively communicated to the audience.

**Inadequate (1 point):** Writing is unclear, distracts from meaning, is not at an appropriate level, or contains excessive errors. Product does not communicate effectively or is not appropriate for the intended audience.

---

### 8. Oral Presentation (Defense)
**Weight:** 5 points maximum
**What is assessed:** The professionalism, organization, and clarity of the oral defense, including use of visual aids and the ability to handle questions thoughtfully and independently.

**Excellent (5 points):** Delivery is highly professional, well-organized, clear, and appropriate for both the project and the audience. Visual aids are used creatively to support the presentation. Student handles Q&A effectively, not only responding to questions but elaborating and expanding on material.

**Adequate (3 points):** Presentation is generally well-organized. Visual aids enhance the presentation. Student is able to respond to Q&A and clearly understands and communicates the material.

**Inadequate (1 point):** Presentation simply follows the order of the paper. Delivery is driven by the PowerPoint. Student is unable to answer questions or requires significant prompting to respond.

---

### 9. Work Ethic and Professionalism
**Weight:** 5 points maximum
**What is assessed:** The student's proactivity, communication, reliability, and adherence to program processes and deadlines throughout the capstone process.

**Excellent (5 points):** Student proactively organizes and schedules meetings with committee and/or client; prepares appropriate forms and documentation needed for discussion and approval.

**Adequate (3 points):** Student communicates with committee and/or client on an as-needed basis; generally meets all deadlines, understands process and requirements, though may rely on committee chair for information about required forms.

**Inadequate (1 point):** Student seldom communicates with chair, committee, or client; misses deadlines; is tardy to meetings; is unaware of university and program policies and procedures.
