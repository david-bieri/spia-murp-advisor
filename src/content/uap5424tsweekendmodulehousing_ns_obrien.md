## Course Description

This module consists of a series of housing data training sessions developed by the Virginia Center for Housing Research (VCHR), covering key data sources and analytical methods used in housing needs assessments and market analysis. Participants work with tools such as the American Community Survey (ACS), Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) earnings data, CHAS affordability data, homeownership market metrics, and the ACS Public Use Microdata Sample (PUMS). Trainings combine instruction, worked examples, and exercises to build applied skills in housing data analysis.

## Learning Objectives

- Define housing markets using geographic and commuting-pattern data from resources such as OnTheMap and Metropolitan/Micropolitan Statistical Areas
- Access, interpret, and analyze American Community Survey (ACS) published tables, including understanding data reliability measures such as Margins of Error and Coefficients of Variation
- Compare BLS earnings data to ACS housing cost data to assess housing affordability by occupation and jurisdiction
- Use CHAS data to conduct a housing affordability gap analysis
- Evaluate homeownership market conditions using key metrics such as median days on market, months of supply, and median sale price
- Work with ACS PUMS household files and R/RStudio to generate customized housing estimates not available in published ACS tables

## Course Topics and Schedule

- **Defining the Housing Market (optional):** Using OnTheMap to examine commuting patterns; overview of Metropolitan and Micropolitan Statistical Areas
- **American Community Survey (ACS):** Housing unit and household characteristic data; data types, reliability, aggregation, and interpretation across Census geographies
- **Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Data:** Comparing earnings to housing costs; affordability calculations; connections to workforce and economic development
- **CHAS Data:** Accessing and interpreting Comprehensive Housing Affordability Strategy data; conducting housing affordability gap analysis
- **Homeownership Market Data:** Key market metrics (days on market, months of supply, median sale price, sales volume); working with REALTOR and assessment datasets
- **PUMS Part 1:** Introduction to ACS Public Use Microdata Sample; R and RStudio basics; generating and assessing reliability of estimates
- **PUMS Part 2 (optional, recommended for experienced data users):** Detailed R coding for customized housing estimates; subsetting PUMS data for analyses not available in published ACS tables

## Advising Notes

This weekend module within UAP 5424 is well suited for MURP students with interests in housing policy, community development, affordable housing, or planning data analytics, as it builds practical skills in the core data tools used in professional housing needs assessments. It complements coursework in housing policy, land use, and quantitative methods and would strengthen students pursuing practicum projects or careers involving housing market analysis. Students with limited prior data experience should note that the PUMS sessions assume some familiarity with data work, and RStudio is required software for those components. The course is delivered as a concentrated weekend module through VCHR; campus, modality, and credit details should be confirmed with the instructor or program coordinator.
