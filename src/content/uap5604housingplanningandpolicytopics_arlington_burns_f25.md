## Course Description
An examination of national and local housing markets with emphasis on the impact of various public housing policies on the demand for and supply of housing within these markets. Explores the relationships between public and private delivery systems. Graduate standing required.

## Learning Objectives
- Provide an overview of housing policy and programs at the federal, state, and local level
- Examine variations in housing types commonly found in the United States and how housing is structured
- Develop familiarity with affordable housing terminology and methods
- Develop a holistic view of the spectrum of housing policy and methods in the United States

## Course Topics and Schedule
- Introduction to housing planning and policy; course expectations
- Why affordable housing matters; overview of the affordable housing problem
- Multifamily housing — private sector; careers in housing and community development
- Multifamily housing — nonprofit sector; Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC)
- Single-family housing; racial homeownership gaps and future of homeownership
- Creative models of homeownership, including Community Land Trusts
- Fair housing law and policy (HUD Fair Housing Act overview)
- Health and housing connections
- Public housing and Section 8 / project-based rental assistance
- Gentrification, urban displacement, and nonprofit models and structures; student presentations and course conclusion

## Advising Notes
This course is well-suited for MURP students with interests in housing policy, community development, affordable housing finance, or social equity planning. It provides broad exposure to the full spectrum of U.S. housing policy — from federal programs like Section 8 and public housing to community land trusts and fair housing law — making it a strong complement to core planning courses and electives in land use or community development. The course features guest lecturers from nonprofit, private, and community development organizations, offering valuable professional networking opportunities.
