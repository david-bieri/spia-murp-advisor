# MURP Elective Course Catalog

Source: SPIA Course_Tracking.xlsx (Spring 2026), spia.vt.edu
Last updated: May 2026 — review at start of each semester

These courses may be used to fulfill the 24-credit MURP elective requirement.
Students should always confirm availability and registration procedures with their advisor.

## Critical Enrollment Caveats

### Natural Resources (NR) Courses
- MURP students can only register for OMNR sections, NOT XMNR sections
- MNR may need to do a campus override to allow MURPs to register
- Contact the MNR department directly to register: confirm current contact via Tyler Wiltshire
- Relevant NR certificates: Natural Resources, Watershed Management, Environmental GIS

### Arlington Students
Most electives are offered online or in Blacksburg. Arlington-based in-person elective
options are very limited. The NR online/asynchronous catalog and GEOG online courses
provide the widest elective access for Arlington students.
