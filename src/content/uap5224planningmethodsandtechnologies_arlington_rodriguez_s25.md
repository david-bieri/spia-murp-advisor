## Course Description
UAP 5224 equips students with data acquisition, data cleaning, and fundamental data analysis skills required for urban planning practice. The course integrates qualitative and quantitative methods to describe facts and derive inferences about planning issues. It also lays a foundation for understanding more complex datasets and analysis strategies critical to successful urban and regional planning research.

## Learning Objectives
- Distinguish qualitative and quantitative data and analytical methods relevant to urban planning
- Identify, retrieve, and prepare data for urban and regional planning analysis
- Apply basic qualitative and quantitative techniques to analyze and present planning-related data
- Conduct and evaluate survey research
- Identify and distinguish planning technologies such as mapping, crowdsourcing, and social networking
- Apply research ethics in data collection, analysis, and reporting

## Course Topics and Schedule
- **Introduction to Data Analysis:** Course overview, types of data, and the role of data in planning practice (Week 1)
- **Data Collection — Sampling and Measurement:** Principles of sampling design and measurement strategies (Weeks 2–3)
- **Survey Design and Research:** Designing and conducting surveys relevant to planning (Week 4)
- **Describing Data with Statistics:** Descriptive statistics and data summarization techniques (Week 5)
- **Working with Census Data:** Accessing, interpreting, and applying U.S. Census datasets to planning contexts (Weeks 6–7)
- **Inferential Statistics and Normal Distributions:** Making inferences from data, introduction to probability and distributions (Weeks 9–10)
- **Hypothesis Testing:** Formulating and testing hypotheses; lab applications (Weeks 11–12)
- **Comparing Groups and Analyzing Frequency Data:** Two-group comparisons and count/frequency analysis methods (Weeks 13–14)
- **Investigating Relationships Between Variables:** Correlation, association, and regression concepts (Week 15)
- **Course Review and Final Examination:** Cumulative review and take-home final exam (Week 16)

## Advising Notes
UAP 5224 is a core methods course designed to build foundational quantitative and qualitative data literacy essential for MURP students across all specializations. It is well-suited for students who are early in their program and seeking to develop practical skills in data collection, statistical analysis, and planning-relevant technologies before advancing to more specialized coursework. The course is offered at the Arlington/NCR campus and taught by Dr. Rodriguez; students at Blacksburg should confirm availability or seek the equivalent section. No formal prerequisites are listed, but students with limited prior exposure to statistics will benefit most from engaging early with the suggested Agresti & Finlay textbook.
