## Course Description
This weekend module is a survey of major housing issues designed to introduce students to the breadth of housing topics they will encounter throughout their studies and careers. Through guest lectures, discussions, readings, and activities, students explore housing development, policy, homelessness, homeownership, financing, data, and fair housing. The format prioritizes active engagement and reflection over traditional deliverables.

## Learning Objectives
- Develop broad awareness of key housing issues relevant to planning practice, including affordability, homelessness, homeownership, and fair housing
- Understand the roles of federal, state, and local housing policy and programs
- Identify and use major housing data sources (e.g., ACS, HMDA, HUD datasets, Virginia-specific resources)
- Engage critically with housing development processes, local regulation, and financing mechanisms
- Connect housing issues to broader urban and regional planning contexts and career practice

## Course Topics and Schedule

- **Introduction to Housing Issues & Why Housing Matters** — framing housing as a planning priority; introductory readings and discussion (Friday afternoon)
- **Housing Development, Construction & Maintenance + Local Regulation** — Guest lecture by Judith Cabelli (Wesley Housing); zoning, building codes, development process
- **Local and State Housing Policy & Programs** — Virginia-specific programs, Housing Forward Virginia, housing trust funds, local strategies
- **Homelessness & Housing Insecurity** — Guest lecture by Tom Barnett (Fairfax County); Point-in-Time data, eviction, student homelessness
- **Homeownership** — Activity on homeownership economics; Guest lecture by Ryan Price (Virginia Association of Realtors); HMDA data
- **Federal Housing Policy & Programs** — HUD programs, GSEs, Low Income Housing Tax Credits, historical and current policy
- **Fair Housing** — Legal frameworks, fair housing complaints data, analysis of impediments
- **Housing Data Overview** — Key datasets (ACS, CHAS, American Housing Survey, OnTheMap); data-driven housing assessments
- **Housing Financing Basics** — Tax credits, bonds, CDFI, Virginia Housing resources
- **Rural Housing Issues** — USDA programs, rural homelessness, Housing Assistance Council resources
- **Flex Topic Block** — Go deeper on a presented topic or explore an additional topic based on class interest (Saturday afternoon)

## Advising Notes
This weekend module is a compact, survey-style introduction to housing issues suitable for MURP students across all concentrations, particularly those interested in housing policy, community development, or social equity planning. It pairs well with more technical housing or data courses in the MURP curriculum, as it provides broad contextual grounding in policy, finance, data, and development before or alongside deeper coursework. The module's guest speakers — practitioners from Wesley Housing, Fairfax County, and the Virginia Association of Realtors — offer strong Northern Virginia and statewide professional exposure. Given references to both Blacksburg resources (Town of Blacksburg Comprehensive Plan) and Arlington/Northern Virginia practitioners, the module appears designed to serve students across VT's campuses.
