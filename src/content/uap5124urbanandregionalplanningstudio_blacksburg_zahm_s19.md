## Course Description
This studio ("application") course builds on knowledge and skills gained in the Theory and History course by applying them to a real problem or project. Students gain knowledge of planning law and legislation, planning processes and participants, and a planner's roles and responsibilities in plan development and implementation. The Spring 2019 studio project focuses on developing a comprehensive plan update for the Town of Fincastle, Virginia, which recently underwent a significant boundary adjustment.

## Learning Objectives
- Develop knowledge of planning law and legislation, including Virginia Code requirements for comprehensive planning
- Understand planning processes, participants, and a planner's roles and responsibilities in plan development and implementation
- Build writing skills for plans, staff reports, technical reports, memos, meeting minutes, and policy statements
- Develop speaking skills including formal presentations, discussion leadership, and meeting facilitation
- Strengthen graphics skills (small- and large-scale; hand-drawn and computer-generated; data, maps, and other visuals)
- Apply data collection and analysis skills using field surveys, interviews, questionnaires, and secondary data sources
- Practice project management skills including scheduling, timetables, and balancing competing project demands

## Required Texts and Materials

## Course Topics and Schedule
- Introduction to the Town of Fincastle project context, client expectations, and studio structure
- Development of a student-created Gantt chart defining tasks, deliverables, responsibilities, and deadlines (early required deliverable)
- Existing conditions documentation: background data collection and GIS mapping for the entire (expanded) Town boundary
- Review and application of Virginia Code requirements for comprehensive planning
- Citizen engagement strategy development, including survey design, visioning session planning, and promotional materials
- Neighborhood and area terminology development in coordination with the Town Planning Commission and Town Council
- Writing assignments: plans, staff reports, technical reports, memos, and/or process summaries
- Graphics production: maps, report graphics, and presentation visuals (hand-drawn and computer-generated)
- Formal and informal presentations to client and peers; practice with speaking and facilitation roles
- Project management and workload coordination throughout the semester

## Advising Notes
UAP 5124 is a core studio course in the MURP program, designed for students who are ready to apply planning theory and technical skills to a live client project — in this case, a comprehensive plan update for the Town of Fincastle, Virginia. The course is particularly well-suited to students interested in comprehensive planning, community engagement, local government planning practice, and GIS/mapping, as all of these skill areas are directly exercised through the studio project. Students should be aware that deadlines are strictly enforced (missed deadlines earn an automatic "F" for that task), and that individual performance within group work is assessed separately, making active contribution throughout the semester essential. This course is offered at the Blacksburg campus and meets once weekly in a three-hour studio block, consistent with the applied, hands-on format expected of a planning studio course.
