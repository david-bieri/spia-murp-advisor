## Course Description
This module introduces students to the basics of the federal, state, and local historic preservation framework, including the National Historic Preservation Act, the National Register of Historic Places, Section 106, and federal preservation tax credits. Students will also learn about local historic designations, architectural review boards, and design guidelines. The module includes advance readings, small group exercises, and a Saturday field trip to historic sites in the Northern Virginia area.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the history and legislative foundation of U.S. historic preservation, including the 1966 National Historic Preservation Act
- Navigate key federal preservation tools: the National Register of Historic Places, Section 106 review, Rehabilitation Tax Credits, and Historic American Building Survey
- Identify the roles of federal, state, and local preservation organizations (SHPOs, Federal Preservation Offices, National Trust, etc.)
- Apply National Register criteria for evaluation to real-world sites and historic districts
- Analyze local historic preservation mechanisms including architectural review boards, design guidelines, and adaptive reuse
- Engage critically with contemporary controversies in historic preservation, such as gentrification, affordability, and recent designations

## Course Topics and Schedule

- **History of U.S. Historic Preservation:** Mt. Vernon, establishment of the National Trust for Historic Preservation (1949), and the National Historic Preservation Act (1966)
- **Federal Preservation Framework:** National Register of Historic Places, National Historic Landmarks, surveys, Section 106, State and Federal Historic Preservation Offices, Rehabilitation Tax Credits, HABS/HAER
- **Preservation Organizations:** National Trust for Historic Preservation; state and local organizations (e.g., Preservation Alliance of Virginia, Waterford Foundation)
- **Rural Historic Districts and Local Preservation:** Architectural Review Boards, design guidelines, adaptive reuse
- **Local Government Development Review:** Preservation, minimization, documentation, and public outreach processes
- **Contemporary Controversies:** Gentrification and "snobism," 50-year designation threshold, guideline strictness and housing affordability impacts
- **Field Trip — Colvin Run Mill:** Tour and discussion of a Fairfax County historic site and its master plan
- **Field Trip — Lake Anne Village:** Lunch at Lake Anne, visit to Reston Museum, and tour/discussion of the Lake Anne Village Historic District
