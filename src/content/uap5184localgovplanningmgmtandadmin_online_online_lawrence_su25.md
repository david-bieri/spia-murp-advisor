## Course Description
This course addresses the roles and responsibilities of a local government planning administrator. It covers planning topics such as leadership, organization and operational management, engagement and collaboration, customer service, conflict management, visioning, politics, and data analytics and technology from a manager's perspective. The course examines these issues as they affect local government administrators, elected officials, citizens, and developers.

## Learning Objectives
- Demonstrate the attributes of a planning manager, including conflict management, problem solving, and decision making
- Respond appropriately to an ethics scenario
- Adapt best practices from other localities to a new locality or department
- Evaluate a planning department and make recommendations for staffing and organization
- Develop performance measures for one or more areas of planning practice
- Review and comment on a departmental budget and/or a locality's capital budget
- Locate and interpret the appropriate state statutes related to a case or scenario
- Organize a process for local visioning and goal setting that engages community and other local, state, and federal stakeholders

## Advising Notes
UAP 5184 is well suited for MURP students interested in local government practice, planning management, or public administration, particularly those who aspire to leadership or administrative roles within planning departments. The course provides a practitioner-focused perspective on departmental management, budgeting, ethics, politics, and visioning that complements more technical or policy-focused MURP coursework. It is offered fully online in Summer II, making it accessible to students at any campus, and is also one of four courses in a Certificate program requiring a B average or better. Students should note that the course draws heavily on real-world case analysis and guest practitioner sessions, making it most valuable for those with some prior exposure to planning concepts or local government contexts.
