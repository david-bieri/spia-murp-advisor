## How Jane should use this file

When a student asks about scheduling conflicts, proposes a semester schedule, or asks
"can I take X and Y together?", check this file. Flag issues clearly but note that most
prereqs can be waived with instructor permission — Jane should not catastrophise.

---

## Core course sequence (official — from murp_course_sequence.md)

Per the official MURP handbook, the standard sequence is:

**Year 1, Fall:** UAP 5014 (Gateway to Planning) · UAP 5234 (Urban Economy) · UAP 5084 (Collaborative Planning)
**Year 1, Spring:** UAP 5174 (Planning Theory & History) · UAP 5554 (Land Use Law) · UAP 5224 (Planning Methods)
**Year 2:** Electives, thesis/capstone, optional certificate courses

*UAP 5174 is a Spring Year 1 course, not Fall. Students who ask about taking 5174 in
Fall should be told it is offered in Spring.*

## Campus availability constraints

Jane must never schedule a Blacksburg-only course for an Arlington student or vice versa.

**Blacksburg-only courses (cannot be taken at Arlington/NCR):**
- UAP 5014 Gateway to Planning (Blacksburg only)
- UAP 5084 Collaborative Planning (Blacksburg only; Arlington has separate version — confirm)
- ARCH courses (5614, 5624, 5644) — Blacksburg only
- LAR courses — Blacksburg only
- Most NR courses — Blacksburg or online; check notes in murp_electives.md

**Arlington/NCR-specific courses:**
- GIA courses — primarily Arlington/NCR
- Some PAPA courses — Arlington/NCR
- Confirm current offerings with Todd Schenk (tschenk@vt.edu) before scheduling

**Both campuses:**
- UAP 5174, UAP 5224, UAP 5234, UAP 5554 — offered both campuses (confirm semester)
- Online courses (see modality in murp_electives.md) — available both campuses

## Common sequencing mistakes Jane should prevent

1. Placing UAP 5174 in Fall Year 1 — it is a Spring course.
2. Placing UAP 5224 Planning Methods in Year 2 — students need it before thesis work begins.
3. Taking NR courses with the XMNR prefix — students must use OMNR (contact NR dept or Kelly Crist).
4. Assuming Arlington students can take ARCH or LAR courses — these are Blacksburg only.
5. Forgetting that CEE 5654 requires CEE 5600 — they cannot be taken concurrently.
6. Certificate course double-counting confusion — courses that satisfy certificate requirements
   can also count toward elective credits. Students do not need extra credits on top of certificates.
