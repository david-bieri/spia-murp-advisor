## Course Description
This 1-credit hour MURP module, titled *Competing with Potholes: Real-World Practices that Advance Sustainability in Local Government*, covers 6 core themes drawn from sustainability practice in local government. Each theme is explored through a practitioner-led presentation, group discussion, and an applied activity. The module is designed to bridge academic planning concepts with the realities of municipal decision-making, communication, and resource management.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the foundational elements of sustainability practice in local government, including the role of comprehensive plans, climate action plans, and climate vulnerability assessments.
- Apply best practices in community engagement, including participatory and consensus-building frameworks (e.g., Arnstein's Ladder).
- Navigate complex decision-making processes using structured models such as weighted criteria analysis.
- Develop facilitation and stakeholder engagement skills, including vision/mission development and managing scope creep.
- Communicate planning work effectively to diverse audiences by translating technical content into accessible formats.
- Understand local government budget structures and apply grant-writing techniques to secure funding for sustainability initiatives.

## Course Topics and Schedule
- **Theme 1 – Introduction to Sustainability Practice in Local Government:** Planning nuts and bolts (What, So What, Now What); competing priorities; goals, objectives & strategies; key planning documents. Activity: BUILT interactive land-use planning game.
- **Theme 2 – Community Engagement:** Best practices, Arnstein's Ladder, participatory design processes. Activity: Playground design exercise.
- **Theme 3 – Complex Decision-Making:** Decision models, weighted criteria analysis, ARPA funding context. Activity: Allocating a $13 million budget.
- **Theme 4 – Facilitation & Stakeholder Engagement:** Consensus-building, defining success, scope creep, transitioning to implementation. Activity: Develop a Vision, Mission & Values document.
- **Theme 5 – Communicating Your Work:** Effective communication principles, audience analysis, translating technical content, accessible reports and presentations. Activity: Critique a state agency report.
- **Theme 6 – Money, Please: Budget Requests & Grant-Writing:** Operational vs. capital budgets, grant funding techniques, grant administration. Activity: Mini-grant writing outline exercise.
