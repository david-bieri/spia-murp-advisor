## How Jane should use this file

When a student asks "what do students like me typically take?" or describes a career
direction and asks what courses others with similar goals have pursued, Jane should find
the closest matching profile and describe it conversationally. Frame these as illustrative
composites, not prescriptions — every student's path is shaped by their own background
and goals.

---

## Profile 1 — Transportation planning, Blacksburg, thesis track

**Background:** Engineering or geography undergraduate. Career goal: state DOT or
federal transportation agency (VDOT, FHWA, FTA).

**Year 1, Fall:** UAP 5014 Gateway to Planning · UAP 5234 Urban Economy · UAP 5084 Collaborative Planning

**Year 1, Spring:** UAP 5174 Planning Theory & History · UAP 5224 Planning Methods · UAP 5554 Land Use Law

**Year 2, Fall:** CEE 5600 Transportation Engineering · CEE 5654 Advanced Transportation · GEOG 5064 GIS · Thesis Research I

**Year 2, Spring:** CEE 5644 New Transportation Topic · UAP elective (equity or housing) · Thesis Research II

**Certificate path:** Transportation and Infrastructure certificate via CEE electives (see murp_certificates_detail.md)

**Jane's note:** Students aiming for VDOT or federal transportation agencies benefit
from the CEE course sequence — it gives technical credibility alongside the planning
framework from the core. UAP 5224 Planning Methods in Year 1 Spring is essential
before thesis work begins; don't delay it.

## Profile 2 — Housing and community development, Arlington, applied project track

**Background:** Social work, public policy, or sociology. Career goal: nonprofit housing
developer, city housing authority, or HUD.

**Year 1, Fall:** UAP 5014 (if available Arlington) · UAP 5234 Urban Economy · GIA elective

**Year 1, Spring:** UAP 5174 Planning Theory & History · UAP 5224 Planning Methods · UAP 5554 Land Use Law

**Year 2, Fall:** PAPA 5784 Community Development · SPIA 5534 Housing Policy · SPIA 5544 Affordable Housing · Applied Project I

**Year 2, Spring:** SPIA 5574 Community Finance · GIA elective · Applied Project II

**Certificate path:** Housing and Community Development certificate via SPIA courses
(see murp_certificates_detail.md)

**Jane's note:** Arlington students have strong access to PAPA and GIA courses that
complement housing and community development work. The DC metro location opens
internship and applied project partnership opportunities with HUD, Enterprise Community
Partners, and housing nonprofits. Kelly Crist (kcrist@vt.edu) is the contact for GA
opportunities.

## Profile 3 — Environmental planning, Blacksburg, thesis track

**Background:** Environmental science, landscape architecture, or biology. Career goal:
local government sustainability office or environmental consulting.

**Year 1, Fall:** UAP 5014 Gateway to Planning · UAP 5234 Urban Economy · UAP 5084 Collaborative Planning

**Year 1, Spring:** UAP 5174 Planning Theory & History · UAP 5224 Planning Methods · UAP 5554 Land Use Law

**Year 2, Fall:** NR 5174 Environmental Policy · NR 5524 Watershed Planning · GEOG 5064 GIS · Thesis Research I

**Year 2, Spring:** NR 5694 Environmental Law · GEOG 5084G Spatial Analysis · Thesis Research II

**Certificate path:** Environmental and Sustainability certificate options — see
murp_certificates_detail.md; also relevant: NR watershed certificate track (NR 5204,
5264, 5274, 5724)

**Jane's note:** NR course enrollment requires using the OMNR prefix, not XMNR —
this is a common administrative snag. Contact Kelly Crist (kcrist@vt.edu) if you hit
enrollment issues. GIS (GEOG 5064) is a high-value early elective for environmental
planning work — it strengthens both thesis methods and job market positioning.

## Profile 4 — International and regional development, Blacksburg or Arlington

**Background:** International studies, economics, or political science. Career goal:
international NGO, World Bank, USAID, or bilateral development organisation.

**Year 1, Fall:** UAP 5014 · UAP 5234 · UAP 5084 (or equivalent)

**Year 1, Spring:** UAP 5174 · UAP 5224 · UAP 5554

**Year 2, Fall:** GIA 5584 Environmental Politics · GIA elective · Regional or housing elective · Thesis Research I

**Year 2, Spring:** GIA elective · NR or SPIA elective · Thesis Research II

**Certificate path:** International development options via GIA (see murp_certificates_detail.md)

**Jane's note:** Students aiming for international careers sometimes feel pressure to
choose a certificate, but in this area it is often better to build breadth across GIA,
PAPA, and NR. Check with Todd Schenk (tschenk@vt.edu) about current GIA course
offerings — GIA course availability varies by semester and campus.

## Profile 5 — Planning analytics, Blacksburg, applied project track

**Background:** Computer science, geography/GIS, or data science. Career goal:
planning analytics role in city government or planning technology firm.

**Year 1, Fall:** UAP 5014 · UAP 5234 · GEOG 5064 GIS (take early — unlocks advanced methods)

**Year 1, Spring:** UAP 5174 · UAP 5224 Planning Methods · UAP 5554

**Year 2, Fall:** GEOG 5245G Advanced GIS · CEE 5600 Transportation · GEOG 5084G Spatial Analysis · Applied Project I

**Year 2, Spring:** CEE 5654 Advanced Transportation · GEOG 5304G Remote Sensing · Applied Project II

**Jane's note:** Taking GIS in Year 1 Fall rather than Year 2 is the single most
important sequencing decision for this profile — GEOG 5245G requires it as a prereq
and the spatial analysis skillset compounds across other electives. Students from CS
backgrounds sometimes underestimate the reading load in UAP 5174; it is theory-heavy.
